# Course Ledger Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** (set at publication)
**Publisher:** Code Cowboys LLC
**Published at:** https://code-cowboys.com/privacy/courseledger

Course Ledger is a personal ledger of golf courses played. Our privacy
policy is short because the app is built so that we *cannot* see your
data.

## The short version

Everything you record in Course Ledger — courses, rounds, scores,
partners, stories, your bucket list — is stored **only on your
device**. Course Ledger has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising,
and makes no network calls. We do not collect, transmit, store, sell,
or share any personal data. We could not hand your ledger to anyone
even if asked, because we never have it.

## Scorecard scanning

The shoebox import photographs your paper scorecards and reads them
**on your device** (Google ML Kit's bundled, offline text
recognition). Card images and everything read from them stay on your
phone. Nothing is uploaded — there is nowhere to upload it to.

## Camera and photos

Camera and photo-library access is used only when you scan scorecards
or attach a photo to a round. Images you capture are stored locally
with the rest of your ledger.

## Location

Course Ledger requests **no location permission**. A course's
coordinates exist only if you type them in yourself, and are stored
locally like everything else.

## Sharing, export, and backup

CSV export and the backup file are created only when you tap the
button, and go only where you send them via your phone's share sheet.
Restoring a backup reads a file you choose. We are never in that path.

## Purchases

Course Ledger Pro is sold through Google Play / the App Store. The
stores process payment and tell the app only "this purchase is owned."
We receive no payment details and keep no purchase records of our own.

## Children

Course Ledger does not knowingly collect data from anyone — including
children — because it collects no data at all.

## Changes

If a future version ever changes what leaves your device, this policy
and its effective date will change with it, and the app will say so
before anything new happens.
